The Unique Anatomy of Spiders

Spiders have a distinctive body structure that is divided into two main parts: the cephalothorax (head and thorax combined) and the abdomen. The cephalothorax contains the brain, eyes, and mouthparts, while the abdomen houses the digestive system and reproductive organs. Spiders also have eight legs, which are attached to the cephalothorax, and chelicerae (jaws) with fangs that can inject venom.
